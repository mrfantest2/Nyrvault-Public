# Nyrvault Visual Target

## Status

**LOCKED TARGET — 2026-09-23**

The CP-007 visual direction is approved. CP-007 remains a future implementation checkpoint; locking the target does not mean the production art pass is complete.

## Identity

Nyrvault's final gameplay presentation is premium modern pixel art designed for portrait Android play. The visual upgrade must preserve the actual destructible cellular mine and free-form tunnels produced by mining.

## Screen

- Android-first portrait presentation.
- Hard pixel edges and nearest-neighbor final assets.
- Primary tile language based around a compact grid.
- Small industrial mining mech and readable autonomous drones.
- Large Core Guardians presented as major set pieces.

## Gameplay composition

The player should always be able to read:

- intact mine tiles;
- excavated empty space;
- the player's mech position and facing;
- autonomous drone activity;
- cyan/gold deposits and rare discoveries;
- sealed technology and deeper objectives;
- compact resource and upgrade information.

Art must enrich the existing gameplay state rather than hide it.

## Shallow Vault art language

- dark graphite/navy engineered stone fused with natural rock;
- cyan crystal deposits with strong phone-scale readability;
- warm gold deposits and circuitry;
- violet ancient-vault glyphs and sealed machinery;
- industrial gold/cyan mech;
- compact autonomous drones;
- buried conduits and technological structures exposed through excavation;
- chunky debris, sparks, ore trails and rare-drop flashes;
- crisp dark UI panels using cyan, gold and violet state colors.

## Moment-to-moment target

Blocks visibly take damage and break into chunky debris. Ore and discoveries travel toward the player. Drones mine independently. Bombs carve obvious geometric pockets. Deeper excavation gradually exposes more architecture and sealed technology so the mine feels increasingly archaeological and mechanically alive.

## Non-goals

- no generic cartoon miner;
- no realistic smooth cave rendering;
- no smooth 3D final sprites;
- no redesign into a conventional side-scrolling platform scene;
- no UI dominated by monetization controls.
